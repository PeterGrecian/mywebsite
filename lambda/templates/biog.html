biography

Contents CV

I read Physics at St Catherine's College, Oxford, then spent several years at Philips Research Labs working on gallium arsenide devices, which produced a handful of scientific papers. Somewhere along the way I was a pavement artist in Paris, painting large copies of old masters in acrylic.

An MSc in Computer Graphics at Middlesex took me into visual effects, where I worked as a generalist at MPC and Method Studios on commercials and feature films (IMDb). That led to freelance work in storage and infrastructure, including a spell at DeepMind in its early days, building high-power compute servers for tuning AI models.

Since then I've built cloud web systems using Infrastructure as Code for clients including TUI and the BMJ, and now work in cloud and DevOps engineering. This past year I've concentrated on using AI efficiently and effectively, and have been through all the pain of developing strategies to build up substantial knowledge about the topic without bloated contexts, so that you don't have to.
