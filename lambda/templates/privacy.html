Who this covers

This site and the tools described below are the personal projects of Peter Grecian. There is no company, no product and no customer base. Nothing here is operated on anyone else's behalf, and none of the data described below is sold, rented, or shared with third parties for advertising or any other purpose.

Google account access (rclone-gdrive)

A Google OAuth client named rclone-gdrive is registered under this project. It exists for one purpose: so that rclone, running on Peter Grecian's own computers, can back up his own files to his own Google Drive.

  • It requests the drive.file scope only. That scope grants access solely to files the application itself creates — it cannot see or touch anything else in the Drive account.
  • It is used by the account owner, on his own machines, against his own Drive. It is not offered as a service to anyone else.
  • Google account data is never transmitted to any server other than Google's own APIs. The OAuth refresh token is held in the rclone configuration file on those machines and in a private secrets store; it is not published, shared, or sent anywhere else.
  • Google user data is not used to train any model, and is not used for advertising, profiling, or resale.

Access can be revoked at any time from Google Account permissions, which immediately invalidates the credential.

What this website records

Requests to www.petergrecian.co.uk are logged for operational reasons — to see what is being used, to measure page timings, and to spot abusive traffic. A log entry may contain the requested path, the timestamp, the originating IP address, the browser user-agent string, and the referring page. These entries are stored in AWS DynamoDB in the eu-west-1 region and are used only to run and debug the site.

The site sets no advertising cookies and runs no third-party analytics or tracking scripts. Some pages are protected by HTTP basic authentication; those credentials are checked against a value held in AWS SSM Parameter Store and are not logged.

Images and cameras

Several pages display images from cameras pointed at the sky or at a private garden in Berrylands, Surrey. Camera pages that could show the garden are password-protected. Sky and timelapse imagery contains no personal data.

Retention and contact

Operational logs are kept only as long as they are useful for diagnostics and are periodically discarded. If you want a log entry associated with you removed, or have any question about this policy, write to peter.grecian@gmail.com and it will be dealt with.
